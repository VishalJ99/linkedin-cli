## 2026-07-14 — PER-379 Railway authentication-gate deployment

### Objective

Deploy the reviewed authentication-only gate to Railway, verify the SQLite
volume and security controls, and stop before the first LinkedIn request if a
deployment prerequisite is unmet.

### Method

- Created the dedicated `linkedin-conversation-finder` Railway project and
  one service.
- Generated the public Railway domain and mounted a 5 GB volume at
  `/app/data`.
- Bound the Chrome connector to the exact Railway origin in commit `2290b3d`.
- Configured the eight gate variables without OpenRouter, MCP, or proxy
  variables; generated independent secrets and kept the invite credential in
  macOS Keychain.
- Deployed with the Railway CLI, exercised health/readiness and invite-session
  endpoints, inspected volume artifacts through Railway SSH, scanned runtime
  logs for configured secrets, and reviewed replica/backup settings in the
  Railway dashboard.

### Observations

- Deployment `70af5d50-3acd-4224-bf36-3a30e9c82642` reached `SUCCESS`.
- `/healthz` returned `{"status":"ok"}` and `/readyz` returned
  `{"status":"ready"}`.
- `/app/data/linkedin_finder.sqlite3` and `/app/data/reproduction.txt` exist;
  reproduction metadata matched the deployed commit and deployment ID.
- Production invite login, authenticated status, and logout succeeded.
- Runtime-log scans found none of the invite credential, Argon2 hash, app
  session secret, or cookie-encryption key and found no fatal markers.
- Railway enforces one replica because the volume is attached.
- Railway's Backups page reports that Backups and point-in-time recovery
  require the Pro plan. A daily schedule could not be configured without a
  billing change.
- An initial login smoke returned a sanitized `422` because a noninteractive
  Keychain command had stored an empty credential. The credential was replaced,
  verified as nonempty before transmission, and the next deployment passed.
  Production form parsing and the application middleware were not at fault.
- No LinkedIn cookie was collected and no LinkedIn endpoint was requested.

### Interpretation

Railway can build and run the gate, preserve SQLite state on the mounted
volume, and serve the invite-protected application without observed secret
leakage. LinkedIn cookie replay remains untested. The approved backup
prerequisite is the current blocker rather than application behavior.
